StreamVault

Your personal streaming vault. All channels, one place.

Stremio — live TV needs VLC Settings → Advanced/Playback → Default Player → External → VLC Your channels: Board (home screen) → “StreamVault Live” row, or Discover → TV channels.
Nuvio — plays live TV in its internal player After installing, restart Nuvio to see your channels. Your channels: Home screen → “StreamVault Live” row.
Setting up for your TV or app
Enter your details below, then Test → Install.
Stremio: on the TV open Addons → Sync addons, then delete the old empty StreamVault.
Other apps: copy the link you get after Install and add it in the app.

Appear after a successful test. Leave empty for all channels.

Ready to install

Your StreamVault keeps the same link, so there's nothing to install again.
Fully close and reopen your app to see the changes — just switching back to it keeps the old categories.
Still the old categories? Remove StreamVault in your app and install it again with the link above — it's the same link, nothing else changes.

Open Stremio

Your app didn't open? Copy the link and paste it in
Stremio → Addons → Install from URL, or Nuvio → Add addon.
Nuvio: restart the app if the channels don't show up.
Have another StreamVault that only shows “Set up StreamVault first”? Delete it from your addons.

Watching on a TV? Install here (phone or PC) while logged in to the same Stremio account, then on the TV open Addons → Sync addons.
