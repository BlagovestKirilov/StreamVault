StreamVault

Live TV from your Xtream or M3U, with TV guide and catch-up.

Stremio — live TV needs VLC Settings → Advanced/Playback → Default Player → External → VLC Your channels: Board (home screen) → “StreamVault Live” row, or Discover → TV channels.
Nuvio — plays live TV in its own player On Samsung (Tizen) TVs, Nuvio is the only way to use StreamVault. After installing, restart Nuvio to see your channels. Your channels: Home screen → “StreamVault Live” row.
